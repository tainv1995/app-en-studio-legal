Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

App EN Studio ("the app") is a self-hosted tool used by its owner to publish English-learning videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): the connected channel's ID and name, and permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, edit metadata and schedule publishing on that channel.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish): the account's open ID, display name and avatar, and permission to upload or post videos to that account.
  • OAuth tokens issued by these platforms so the app can act on the connected account.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, messages, contacts or analytics of other users.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to upload and schedule videos the owner has approved, and to show the connection status in the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

3. Storage and security

  • Tokens and API keys are stored in the app's own database on the owner's computer, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • They are never logged, shown in full in the interface, or sent anywhere other than the platform that issued them.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share user data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (Google/YouTube or TikTok) when performing an action the owner requested.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

App EN Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected, then deleted from the database.
  • You can disconnect inside the app at any time, or revoke access at Google Account permissions or in TikTok's "Manage app permissions".
  • To request deletion of any stored data, email the address below; requests are handled within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is operated by an adult owner. Some videos are made for children, but the app does not collect any data from children or viewers.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
