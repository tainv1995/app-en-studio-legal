App EN Studio

A private production tool for English-learning videos.

App EN Studio helps one creator produce short English lessons for YouTube and TikTok. It writes a lesson script with AI, has a person review it, adds a synthetic voice, renders the video, and has a person review the video again before it is published.

What the app does with your accounts

  • YouTube: uploads videos the operator has approved to the operator's own channel, sets title, description, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.
  • TikTok: sends approved short videos to the operator's own TikTok account (inbox draft or post).
  • It does not read, collect or analyse other people's data, and it is not offered to the public.

Who can use it

App EN Studio is a self-hosted tool run by its owner. Only the owner signs in and only the owner's own channels are connected.
